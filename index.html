About Pip

Croissants first.
Science second.

I trained as a pastry chef in Melbourne and spent most of my twenties laminating butter at 4am. I loved it, but I kept noticing that the people buying my pastries apologised for eating them. So I went back to uni, became a dietitian, and made it my job to help people stop apologising.

These days I see clients in a sunny room above a bakery on South Terrace (yes, on purpose) and online across Australia. I'm evidence-based, allergic to fads, and I'll never hand you a meal plan that bans your nan's lasagne.

Questions

The bits people actually ask.

Will you weigh me?

Only if you want me to and there's a clinical reason. Most of my clients never step on a scale with me.

Do I need a referral?

No. If you have a GP Chronic Disease Management plan you can claim a Medicare rebate. Most private health funds cover dietetics too.

Can I really keep eating bread?

Yes. Even if we do low FODMAP for a while, sourdough spelt is usually on the menu. I checked.

Is Biscuit available for consults?

He is available for pats before and after. He is not qualified to give nutrition advice, despite what he thinks.
