How this site became Pip's.

Pip Marlow is a made-up client: a non-diet dietitian in Fremantle who spent nine years as a pastry chef, has a dry sense of humour and a retired greyhound called Biscuit. The brief was a homepage that feels like meeting her. I explored two moodboard directions before designing a single section.

Design notes

What makes it hers

One memorable idea

The food rules section. Each diet myth gets crossed out as you scroll, with Pip's reply scribbled beside it. It says "non-diet dietitian" without anyone needing to read that phrase.

Details only Pip could have

Her sourdough starter has a name (Gary). Services are written as recipe cards. Testimonials live on a fridge. The footer is signed off by Biscuit.

Colour with a point of view

Tomato, olive and butter instead of the usual clinic mint and white. Every pairing used for text passes WCAG AA contrast, and body copy stays 17px or larger on phones.

Space used on purpose

A huge, confident headline, generous gaps between sections, and one tilted element per section so the page feels handmade rather than templated.

Built for a Webflow handover

Sections are self-contained and reusable (hero, rule list, polaroid about, recipe cards, steps, notes, FAQ, CTA), so the other pages can be built from them. In Figma these would be components with named layers and shared styles.

Small, kind motion

Steam rising off the loaf, Biscuit's ear twitch, counters and gentle reveals. All of it switches off for people who prefer reduced motion.
